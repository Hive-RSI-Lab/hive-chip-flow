# boundary_incomplete (boundary, refused)

The `boundary:` section or the netlist cannot be measured as declared: a
crossing pin missing or under the wrong direction, a pin with no static
level, a net to observe that is not in the netlist, or a `pin:` load the
liberty does not have. The message names which.

**Cheapest fix first:** correct the declaration. Bus bits are written
`name[i]`, one entry each. `open` leaves a pin unconnected on purpose.
